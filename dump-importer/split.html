WebRTC Dump Splitter

Split a dump that spans several sites and keep only the origins you want to share.

Accepts webrtc-internals JSON dumps and rtcstats dumps (.json, .jsonl, .txt, .gz)

Origins in this dump

Everything runs locally in your browser - no data leaves your machine. To strip IP addresses as well, run the result through the dump anonymizer.

Split first, then analyze

A dump narrowed down to one origin is quicker to upload and makes the analysis on rtcstats.com more focused, with interactive visualizations, automatic analysis, and debugging tools for your WebRTC data - so you can find the problem instead of reading raw JSON.
